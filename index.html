💡 CRITICAL ALGORITHMIC LESSON

DFS does NOT automatically choose the shortest or lowest-cost path.
The route travel times in this game were used purely for gameplay time pressure, not as the decision-making rule for Depth First Search. DFS explores deeply along branches regardless of travel cost.

How To Play

Gameplay & Controls

  • Use WASD or Arrow Keys to move your wizard.
  • Follow the currently unlocked magical path.
  • Collect the magical keys in the maze.
  • A Dementor appears when you complete a new dead-end branch.
  • Backtrack quickly to escape it!
  • New DFS branches unlock as you progress.
  • Complete the required exploration and reach the final door before time runs out.
  • The Dark Lord catches you if the timer reaches zero.
  • Watch the live graph while you explore.
